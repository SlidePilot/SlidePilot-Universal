# SlidePilot privacy policy

Effective for SlidePilot 2.0 and later on Mac, iPad and iPhone.

**SlidePilot collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server.

## What stays on your device
- The PDFs you open are read from where you keep them. SlidePilot does not upload or copy them anywhere.
- Your notes, drawings and per-document settings are stored in SlidePilot's own storage on your device. They leave it only when you export them yourself as a `.slidepilot` or PDF file.
- Settings such as the layout and the pointer are stored on your device.

## Remote control
When you use remote control, SlidePilot connects two of your devices directly over the local network. Slide images, notes and commands are sent only between these two devices, encrypted, and never through a server. The devices find each other with Bonjour and show their device names to each other. The keys of paired devices are stored in your device's keychain.

## Purchases
The in-app purchase SlidePilot Pro is handled by Apple's App Store. SlidePilot only asks the App Store whether the purchase exists on your Apple Account; it receives no payment details.

## Crash reports
If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to the developer. SlidePilot itself sends nothing.

## Contact
Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/SlidePilot/SlidePilot-Universal/issues>.
