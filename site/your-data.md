# Your Data

SlidePilot stores your **notes**, **drawings** (with Save drawings on) and the **settings of each PDF** (layout, notes mode, pointer) by itself. It saves every change right away. Nothing is written into the PDF or next to it.

## Moving and renaming PDFs

The data stays with the PDF when you rename or move it, and when LaTeX replaces it. If SlidePilot cannot find the PDF any more, for example because you copied it to another drive or downloaded it again, it asks when you open the new copy: **Use Data from a Missing PDF?**

## Moving data to another device

The data stays on the device. To take it to another device, or to keep a copy, export a **SlidePilot file**:

- Mac: **File ▸ Export SlidePilot File…**
- iPad and iPhone: **Import and Export ▸ Export SlidePilot File…** in the toolbar

Open the file on the other device (for example with AirDrop or from Files) to import it into the matching PDF. SlidePilot asks before it replaces existing notes or drawings, and warns if the file was made for a PDF with another name or number of pages. The PDF itself is not part of the file.

## Stored Document Data

**Settings ▸ General ▸ Stored Document Data…** lists the data of every PDF: what it holds, when the PDF was last opened, and **PDF not found** when SlidePilot cannot find the PDF. From there you can export an entry as a SlidePilot file, or delete entries. SlidePilot never deletes notes or drawings by itself; settings of PDFs without notes or drawings are removed after 12 months.
