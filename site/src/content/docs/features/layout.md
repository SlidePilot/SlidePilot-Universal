title: Layout

The presenter view shows up to three slots: the **current slide**, the **next slide** and your **notes**.

## Arrangement

Choose the arrangement in the toolbar's **Layout** menu, or on the Mac also in **View ▸ Arrangement**:

- **Single Slide**
- **Two Slides**
- **Three Slides, Large Left** or **Large Right**: one large slot and two small ones

Below the arrangement, the same menu has one picker per slot (for example Left, Top Right, Bottom Right) to choose what each slot shows. Choosing something that is already shown in another slot swaps the two.

To show or hide a single slot quickly:

| Slot | Key |
|---|---|
| Current slide | A |
| Next slide | P |
| Notes | N |

Drag the line between two slots to change their size. In a portrait or narrow window, the slots are stacked instead of side by side.

SlidePilot remembers the layout for each PDF. A PDF you open for the first time gets the layout you used last.

After the last slide, the next-slide slot says **End of Presentation**, so you know there is nothing more to come.

## Slide navigator

The navigator is a sidebar with all slides. Open it with **O**, the **Show Navigator** button in the toolbar, or **View ▸ Show Navigator**; close it with the same button or Esc.

- Click or tap a slide to show it.
- To jump to a slide by number, type the number and press Return. On iPad and iPhone, type it into the page field above the slides.
- On the Mac and iPad, drag the edge of the sidebar to make it wider. On iPhone and in narrow iPad windows, the navigator opens as a sheet and closes when you choose a slide.

## Layout settings

**Settings ▸ Layout** applies to all PDFs:

- **Slide padding**: the space around each slide (None, Small, Normal).
- **Time display**: the size of the clock and timer above the slides (Hidden, Small, Normal).

**Settings ▸ General ▸ Crossfade slides** lets the slides fade into each other on the presentation screen. It is off while Reduce Motion is on.
