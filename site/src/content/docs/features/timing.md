---
title: Timing
---

Above the slides, the presenter view shows the **time** of your talk on the left and the **clock** on the right. The clock follows your system's 12- or 24-hour setting.

## Stopwatch

By default the time is a stopwatch that counts up from 00:00:00.

- **It starts by itself** the first time you go to the next slide with the keyboard, a clicker or the menu. Swipes, the navigator, links and remote devices don't start it.
- **Start/Stop** with **S** or the **Time** button in the toolbar. Once you start or stop it yourself, it no longer starts by itself.
- **Reset** with **R** or **Time ▸ Reset Time**.

## Timer

To count down instead, choose **Set Timer…** (**T**, or in the **Time** menu) and enter hours, minutes and seconds. The timer starts like the stopwatch.

- After zero, the timer keeps counting as overtime, shown in red with a minus sign.
- **Reset** goes back to the time you set.
- To change the time, choose **Set Timer…** again, even while it runs.
- **Turn Off Timer** (or setting 00:00:00) goes back to the stopwatch.

### Warnings before the end

The timer changes color as the end comes closer: **orange** from the first warning, **purple** from the second, **red** in overtime. Choose when in **Time ▸ First Warning** and **Second Warning**: Off or 1 to 30 minutes before the end (default: 5 and 1 minute). With **Differentiate Without Color** turned on in the Accessibility settings, the timer also shows one or two exclamation marks.

Every PDF window has its own stopwatch or timer.

The size of the time display, or hiding it, is set in **Settings ▸ Layout ▸ Time display**. On iPad and iPhone, the time is hidden while you draw, so the slide gets more room.
