title: Settings

Open the settings with **SlidePilot ▸ Settings…** (⌘,) on the Mac, or the **Settings** button in the toolbar on iPad and iPhone.

| Pane | Settings |
|---|---|
| General | [SlidePilot Pro](/pro/), Appearance (System, Light, Dark), [Keep display awake](/features/presenting/#keeping-the-display-awake), [Crossfade slides](/features/layout/#layout-settings), [Save drawings](/features/drawing/#keeping-drawings), [Stored Document Data…](/your-data/#stored-document-data) |
| Layout | [Slide padding and time display](/features/layout/#layout-settings) |
| Pointer | The [Individual pointer](/features/pointer/#appearance) |
| Remote | [Allow remote control and trusted devices](/features/remote/#connecting) |

## Opening this help

On the Mac, choose **Help ▸ SlidePilot Help** (⌘?). On iPad and iPhone, tap **SlidePilot Help** at the end of Settings.
