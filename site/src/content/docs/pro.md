---
title: SlidePilot Pro
---

SlidePilot is free to download from the App Store. Everything on your own device is unlimited: opening PDFs, the presenter view, notes, timer, pointer, drawing, curtain and settings.

**SlidePilot Pro** is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks without time limit:

- **Presenting to the audience**: the presentation on a second screen, an external display or AirPlay.
- **Remote control**: controlling another device and being controlled.

## Trying it

Without Pro, each of these runs as a trial of 3 minutes:

- The audience screen shows **SlidePilot – Trial Version** during the trial. After 3 minutes it shows **Trial Ended** instead of the slides. Start the presentation or connect the display again for another trial.
- A remote connection ends after 3 minutes. Connect again for another trial.

## Buying and restoring

Choose **Unlock Pro** in the hint at the top of the presenter view, or go to the **SlidePilot Pro** section in Settings (Mac: **Settings ▸ General**). One purchase works on all your Macs, iPads and iPhones with the same Apple Account, and can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

On a new device, or after reinstalling, choose **Restore Purchases** in the same section.
