## SlidePilot 2.0

A new SlidePilot, written from scratch for macOS 26, iPadOS 26 and iOS 26.

- **iPad and iPhone**: present with an external display or AirPlay, draw with Apple Pencil, point with a double tap.
- **Remote control between any devices**: iPhone, iPad and Mac control each other, paired with a code and encrypted. Replaces the SlidePilot Remote app.
- **Notes and drawings are saved automatically** with each PDF, and move with it; export and import them as a SlidePilot file.
- **Several PDFs at once**, each in its own windows.
- **Timer warnings** in orange and purple before the end.
- **Videos** from Beamer with autostart, loop, start and duration, also across overlay steps and stored inside the PDF.
- **Layout per PDF**, chosen right in the toolbar.
- Light and dark mode, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type; English, German and French.
- Free on the App Store, with [SlidePilot Pro](/pro/) for presenting to an audience and remote control without time limit.
