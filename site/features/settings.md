# Settings

Open the settings with **SlidePilot ▸ Settings…** (⌘,) on the Mac, or the **Settings** button in the toolbar on iPad and iPhone.

| Pane | Settings |
|---|---|
| General | [SlidePilot Pro](../pro.md), Appearance (System, Light, Dark), [Keep display awake](presenting.md#keeping-the-display-awake), [Crossfade slides](layout.md#layout-settings), [Save drawings](drawing.md#keeping-drawings), [Stored Document Data…](../your-data.md#stored-document-data) |
| Layout | [Slide padding and time display](layout.md#layout-settings) |
| Pointer | The [Individual pointer](pointer.md#appearance) |
| Remote | [Allow remote control and trusted devices](remote.md#connecting) |

## Opening this help

On the Mac, choose **Help ▸ SlidePilot Help** (⌘?). On iPad and iPhone, tap **SlidePilot Help** at the end of Settings.
